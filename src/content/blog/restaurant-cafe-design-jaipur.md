A restaurant or cafe has a few seconds to make an impression, and then it has to work smoothly for hours every day. Good hospitality design balances both. These are the points we work through with restaurant and cafe owners.

## Start with a clear idea

Every memorable place has one strong idea: a colour, a material, a story about the city or the food. In Jaipur that might be a modern take on Rajasthani arches and jaali, or something completely contemporary. A clear concept guides every later choice and makes the space easy to recognise and photograph.

## The front draws people in

The facade, the signage and the view inside sell the place before anyone reads the menu. Glass that shows a busy, well-lit interior and a sign that is lit well at night do a lot of work.

## Layout and seating

Mix seating types: booths and banquettes for groups, two-tops that can join, and bar or counter seats for people on their own. Keep clear routes for staff between the kitchen and every table, and plan where people wait at busy times.

## Kitchen and service flow

The kitchen size, the pass, washing, storage and deliveries need to be planned with the chef early. A beautiful dining room cannot make up for a kitchen that slows service down.

## Lighting and sound

Warm, layered lighting sets the mood and makes food look good. Hard surfaces like tile and glass get loud quickly, so balance them with fabric, timber and acoustic ceilings.

## Durable materials

Floors, tabletops and upholstery take heavy daily use. Choose finishes that are easy to clean and repair, and that still look good after a year of service.
