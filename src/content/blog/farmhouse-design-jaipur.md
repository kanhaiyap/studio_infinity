A farmhouse is usually a weekend home, a place for family gatherings and a garden in one. That makes it a different brief from a city house, and worth planning carefully before construction starts.

## Start with the site

Walk the land at different times of day. Note the existing trees, the slope, where water collects after rain, the views worth keeping and the direction of the summer wind. A good farmhouse design keeps the best of what is already there.

## Design for the Rajasthan climate

Summers around Jaipur are long and hot. Deep verandas, courtyards, shaded openings, thick walls and cross-ventilation keep a farmhouse comfortable with far less air conditioning.

## Plan for gatherings

Farmhouses tend to fill up on weekends and festivals. Generous living and dining spaces that open onto lawns and terraces, guest rooms with their own bathrooms, and a kitchen that can cater for a crowd all make a difference.

## Landscape is half the project

Lawns, trees, a pool, paths and outdoor seating are what you will use most. Designing them with the house, not after it, gives a farmhouse that feels complete.

## From design to construction

A farmhouse architect handles the site study, design, 3D views, working drawings and approvals, and stays involved through construction so the finished farmhouse matches the design.
