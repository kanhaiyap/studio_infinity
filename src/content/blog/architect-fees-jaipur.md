"What does an architect cost?" is usually the first question we hear, and the honest answer is that it depends on the project. Here is how fees are usually worked out, so you can compare quotes on equal terms.

## How architects charge

- **A percentage of construction cost.** The most common method for new homes and buildings. The fee grows with the size and budget of the project.
- **A rate per square foot.** Common for interiors and for smaller, well-defined projects.
- **A fixed lump sum.** Used when the scope is clear from the start, such as a house plan and elevation only.

## What changes the fee

- **Scope.** A house plan alone costs far less than a full service with working drawings, approvals, interiors and site supervision.
- **Size and complexity.** A farmhouse, villa or hotel with custom details takes more design time than a simple plan.
- **Interiors and furniture.** Custom joinery, furniture and lighting design add drawings and site visits.
- **Site visits.** Projects outside Jaipur, or with frequent visits, cost more to supervise.

## What a full service includes

Site study, concept design and 3D views, the house plan and front elevation, working drawings for the contractor, drawings for building plan approval, and visits to site until handover. When you compare quotes, check which of these each one covers.

## Interior design cost

Interior fees are separate from the cost of the work itself: materials, furniture, lighting and labour. A clear design and drawings help keep that second number under control, because the contractor knows exactly what to build.
