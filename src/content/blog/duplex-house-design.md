A duplex gives a family two floors joined by an internal staircase. It is one of the most popular house types on Jaipur plots, and it works best when both floors are planned together from the start.

## Decide what goes on each floor

The usual split is shared spaces below and private spaces above: living, dining, kitchen and a guest or parents' bedroom on the ground floor, and the other bedrooms and a family lounge upstairs. If elderly family members live with you, a full bedroom suite on the ground floor is worth the space.

## The staircase is the centrepiece

In a duplex the staircase links everything, so its position shapes the whole plan. Placed near the centre, it keeps corridors short. Next to a double-height space or a skylight, it brings light down into the middle of the house and becomes a feature in its own right.

## Privacy and the living room

Guests should be able to reach the living room without passing the bedrooms or the kitchen. A separate formal living room and an informal family lounge upstairs give the household space to relax while visitors are over.

## Parking and the ground floor

Parking often takes a large share of the ground floor. Decide early how many cars you need to park inside the plot, because it affects the entrance, the front elevation and the size of the rooms behind.

## Plan for later

Families change. Leave the structure ready for another floor, keep a room that can become a study or a bedroom, and plan service shafts and plumbing so that later changes do not mean breaking floors.

## Drawings that help

A clear house plan, a 3D model and a front elevation let you walk through both floors before construction starts. Changes are cheap on paper and expensive on site.
