Your interiors are the part of the home you touch and use every day, so the person who designs them matters. Here is what to look at when you compare interior designers in Jaipur.

## Designer or decorator?

An interior decorator mainly chooses finishes, furniture and soft furnishings for existing rooms. An interior designer also works on the layout: lighting, electrical points, false ceilings, built-in joinery, kitchens and bathrooms, with drawings that a contractor can build from. For a new home or a full renovation, you need a designer.

## Look at finished work, not only renders

3D views show the intention. Photographs of completed projects show whether the studio can deliver it on site. Ask to see both, and ask which renders were actually built.

## Check the range

A good portfolio shows different moods and budgets, not the same room repeated. Look for details you care about: storage, lighting, the way materials meet, how kitchens and bathrooms are handled.

## Questions to ask

- **What is included?** Concept, 3D views, working drawings, material selection, site visits, or full execution.
- **How is the fee worked out?** By area, as a percentage, or as a fixed sum. See our post on [architect fees in Jaipur](../architect-fees-jaipur/).
- **Who supervises the site?** And how often does someone visit.
- **Is furniture custom or bought?** Custom pieces fit better but take longer.

## Architect and interior designer together

When the same studio designs the house and the interiors, the lighting, ceilings, windows and furniture are planned together from the start. That saves changes on site and gives a home that feels like one idea.
