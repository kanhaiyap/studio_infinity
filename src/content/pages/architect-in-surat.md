Studio Infinity is an architecture and interior design firm working in Surat. We design luxury homes, villas, farmhouses and interiors for families, and hotels, restaurants and offices for businesses, across Surat and Gujarat.

## Homes, villas and farmhouses

From a modern house plan and front elevation to a full luxury villa or farmhouse, we design homes around the site, Surat's humid coastal climate and the way you live. Cross-ventilation, raised plinths and materials that stand up to humidity are planned in from the start. We share 3D views at every stage, so you can see the house before it is built.

## Interiors

As interior designers in Surat, we plan complete interiors for bungalows, apartments and penthouses: layout, materials, lighting, custom joinery and furniture, carried through to handover.

## Hotels, restaurants and offices

We design boutique hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafes, offices and showrooms in Surat and across Gujarat.

## How we work

We visit the site, understand your brief and budget, and take the project from concept design through detailed drawings to coordination on site.
