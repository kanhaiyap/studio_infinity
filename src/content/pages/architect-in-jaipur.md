Studio Infinity is an architecture firm in Jaipur, led by Ar. Shivang Agarwal and Ar. Divya Gupta. We work as architects and interior designers on luxury homes, villas and farmhouses for families, and on hotels, resorts and restaurants for businesses, across Jaipur and Rajasthan.

## Luxury homes, villas and farmhouses

As a luxury architect in Jaipur, we design everything from a modern house plan and front elevation to a full villa or farmhouse, around the site, Jaipur's hot, dry climate and the way you live. We share 3D views at every stage, so you can see the house before it is built.

## Interiors and furniture

As interior designers in Jaipur, we plan complete interiors for bungalows, apartments and penthouses: layout, materials, lighting, custom furniture and joinery, carried through to handover. Gardens, courtyards and terraces are designed with the house.

## Hotel, resort and heritage restoration architects

We are hotel and resort architects in Rajasthan, designing boutique hotels, resorts, restaurants and cafes. As heritage restoration architects, we restore havelis and heritage properties, giving old buildings a new use while keeping their character.

## Where we work in Jaipur

Our studio is at 117, Heera Nagar, DCM, Ajmer Road. We work across the city, including Mansarovar, Vaishali Nagar, Jagatpura, C-Scheme and Civil Lines, and take on villa, farmhouse and resort projects across Rajasthan and beyond.

## How we work

We visit the site, understand your brief and budget, and take the project from concept design through detailed drawings to coordination on site.
