Studio Infinity is an architecture and interior design firm working in Hyderabad. We design luxury homes, villas, farmhouses and interiors for families, and hotels, restaurants and offices for businesses, across Hyderabad and Telangana.

## Homes, villas and farmhouses

From a modern house plan and front elevation to a full luxury villa or farmhouse, we design homes around the site, Hyderabad's hot summers and rocky terrain and the way you live. Shaded openings, thermal mass and plans that work with the rocky ground are planned in from the start. We share 3D views at every stage, so you can see the house before it is built.

## Interiors

As interior designers in Hyderabad, we plan complete interiors for bungalows, apartments and penthouses: layout, materials, lighting, custom joinery and furniture, carried through to handover.

## Hotels, restaurants and offices

We design boutique hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafes, offices and showrooms in Hyderabad and across Telangana.

## How we work

We visit the site, understand your brief and budget, and take the project from concept design through detailed drawings to coordination on site.
