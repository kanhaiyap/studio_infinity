Studio Infinity is an architecture and interior design firm working in Ahmedabad. We design luxury homes, villas, farmhouses and interiors for families, and hotels, restaurants and offices for businesses, across Ahmedabad and Gujarat.

## Homes, villas and farmhouses

From a modern house plan and front elevation to a full luxury villa or farmhouse, we design homes around the site, Ahmedabad's long, hot summers and the way you live. Shaded courtyards, deep verandas and cross-ventilation keep homes cool through the summer. We share 3D views at every stage, so you can see the house before it is built.

## Interiors

As interior designers in Ahmedabad, we plan complete interiors for bungalows, apartments and penthouses: layout, materials, lighting, custom joinery and furniture, carried through to handover.

## Hotels, restaurants and offices

We design boutique hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafes, offices and showrooms in Ahmedabad and across Gujarat.

## How we work

We visit the site, understand your brief and budget, and take the project from concept design through detailed drawings to coordination on site.
