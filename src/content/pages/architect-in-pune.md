Studio Infinite is an architecture and interior design firm working in Pune. We design luxury homes, villas, farmhouses and interiors for families, and hotels, restaurants and offices for businesses, across Pune and Maharashtra.

## Homes, villas and farmhouses

From a modern house plan and front elevation to a full luxury villa or farmhouse, we design homes around the site, Pune's mild climate and heavy monsoon and the way you live. Wide overhangs, good drainage and rooms that open up in the cooler months make the most of the climate. We share 3D views at every stage, so you can see the house before it is built.

## Interiors

As interior designers in Pune, we plan complete interiors for bungalows, apartments and penthouses: layout, materials, lighting, custom joinery and furniture, carried through to handover.

## Hotels, restaurants and offices

We design boutique hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafes, offices and showrooms in Pune and across Maharashtra.

## How we work

We visit the site, understand your brief and budget, and take the project from concept design through detailed drawings to coordination on site.
