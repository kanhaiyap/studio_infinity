We are residential architects in Ahmedabad, designing modern houses, duplexes, bungalows and apartment buildings. Each one is planned around the site, Ahmedabad's long, hot summers and the way the people living there use their space.

## What we design

- Modern houses and independent homes
- Duplex houses and bungalows
- Apartment and residential buildings
- Renovations and extensions

## House plan, elevation and 3D design

Every project starts with the house plan: room layouts, light, ventilation and Vastu where you want it. We then design the front elevation and share 3D views, so you can see the finished house before construction begins, and prepare the working drawings for approvals and the contractor.
