Studio Infinite is an architecture and interior design firm based in Jaipur. We design luxury homes, villas, farmhouses, hotels and workplaces, and we carry each project from the first conversation through to completion on site.

## Principal architects

The studio is led by **Ar. Shivang Agarwal** and **Ar. Divya Gupta**, who work on every project from the first site visit to handover.

## What we do

- **Architecture.** Homes, villas, farmhouses, hotels, resorts and commercial buildings.
- **Interiors.** Complete interiors for homes, offices, restaurants and showrooms.
- **Furniture design.** Custom furniture and joinery made for each space.
- **Landscape.** Gardens, courtyards and outdoor spaces designed with the building.

## How we work

1. **Brief and site study.** We understand your needs, the site and the constraints.
2. **Concept design.** We explore layouts, massing and materials, and agree on a direction with you.
3. **Detailed design.** We prepare drawings, specifications and approvals.
4. **Execution.** We coordinate with contractors and consultants and stay involved on site until handover.
