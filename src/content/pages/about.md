Studio Infinity is an architecture and interior design practice. We design homes, workplaces and commercial spaces, and we carry each project from the first conversation through to completion on site.

## How we work

1. **Brief and site study.** We understand your needs, the site and the constraints.
2. **Concept design.** We explore layouts, massing and materials, and agree on a direction with you.
3. **Detailed design.** We prepare drawings, specifications and approvals.
4. **Execution.** We coordinate with contractors and consultants and stay involved on site until handover.
